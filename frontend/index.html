// how it works

Welcome to Bike Service Management

The platform automates the process of scheduling, tracking, and managing bike repairs — from the moment you add a bike to the moment the mechanic marks it complete and paid.

  • Register and add your bikes to your account
  • Book a service slot for any bike, any date
  • Admins approve the booking and take it into the shop
  • Pay by wallet or cash once the service is complete
